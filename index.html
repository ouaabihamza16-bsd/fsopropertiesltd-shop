About us

A small landlord company in Telford

FSO Properties Ltd is a private limited company based in Telford, Shropshire. It was incorporated in England and Wales on 18 June 2020 under company number 12680366.

The company owns residential homes and lets them to private tenants itself. It is a landlord, not a letting agent or managing agent: it does not let or manage property on behalf of other owners.

Because the company owns the homes it lets, tenants deal with their landlord directly. The tenancy is with FSO Properties Ltd, and the company looks after the upkeep of its own homes.

What we do

Homes we own, let by us

Our work is simple: we own residential homes and rent them out ourselves. Here is what that means for the people who live in them.

Residential homes to rent

FSO Properties Ltd owns the homes it lets and has held residential property since 2020. Every home we let is one the company owns itself, rented out as a place for people to live.

Tenancies direct with the owner

Your tenancy is with the company that owns your home, not with a third-party letting agent. You know who your landlord is and who to contact about your home.

Repairs and upkeep

As the owner and landlord, the company is responsible for the repairs and upkeep of the homes it lets, and it arranges this work for its own properties.

Why rent from us

Owner and landlord, one company

  • No agent in the middle

    The company that owns your home is the company you rent it from.

  • Clear responsibility

    As owner and landlord, the company itself is responsible for the homes it lets.

  • A registered company

    FSO Properties Ltd is registered at Companies House in England and Wales, so its details are on the public register.
